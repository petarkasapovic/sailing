# Sailaway III

**Sailaway III** offers a vast, realistic world map for epic voyages with better graphics, while **eSail** focuses on detailed, hands-on learning with interactive lessons and crew management, featuring a more intuitive UI but older-looking graphics; choose Sailaway for world exploration and eSail for learning the specifics of boat handling and docking. Both aim for realism but cater to different priorities, with eSail being praised for its training and Sailaway for its scope, though Sailaway's business practices and eSail's engine get mixed reviews.

**Sailaway III**

  * **Pros:** Vast world, real-world weather, better graphics (than eSail v1/v2), ability to sail around the world, dynamic wind shifts, support for multiple boat types (via DLC).

  * **Cons:** Empty world (no shipping), UI can be clunky, graphics still "indie" close to land, microtransactions for boats.
* **Best For:** Long-distance cruising, exploring the whole planet, experiencing real-world weather and routing. 

**eSail Sailing Simulator**

  * **Pros:** Excellent for learning (tutorials, docking, anchoring), intuitive UI, dedicated community, multiplayer, NPC boats, realistic sail/rudder physics.
  * **Cons:** Smaller world, older graphics engine (though improving), lacks some advanced features like spinnakers (historically, though improving).
  * **Best For:** New sailors, hands-on training, mastering boat handling, realistic docking/anchoring. 

**Key Differences**
  * **Focus:** Sailaway = big picture sailing/navigation; eSail = detailed boat management/training.
  * **World:** Sailaway = entire planet; eSail = detailed, smaller areas.
  * **Learning:** eSail has built-in tutorials; Sailaway is more about applying learned knowledge. 

**Which to Choose?**

  * **For absolute beginners wanting to learn:** Start with **eSail** for its structured learning.
  * **For experienced sailors wanting a virtual "around the world" trip:** Choose **Sailaway III** for its massive map and weather.
  * **For a balanced experience:** Consider both; eSail for learning, Sailaway for cruising, though many prefer eSail's realism and community.
