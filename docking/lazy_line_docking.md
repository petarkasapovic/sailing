# Lazy line

A "port lazy line" (or just lazy line) is a pre-set mooring rope in harbors, especially for stern-to docking, that lets boats easily secure their bows without dropping anchor, consisting of a heavy line from a seabed block to a lighter messenger line on the dock, making it simple to grab, pull forward, and tie off, keeping boats tidy and secure in busy spots like the Mediterranean.
